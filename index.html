Set it up. Let it perch.

  1. Move Relay to Applications. Open the app and choose your usual browser.
  2. Set Relay as your default browser. macOS will ask you to confirm.
  3. Look for the owl. Change where links open from the menu bar, whenever you need.

Your browsers do the browsing. Relay routes links locally and keeps no browsing history. Update checks contact GitHub for new releases.
